# British Informatics Olympiad Solutions
When practising for the British Informatics Olympiad, it is hard to come across solutions to past problems. It is even harder to come across solutions to these problems that have adequate documentation. 

This makes it incredibly difficult for beginners who do not already have a firm grasp of competitive programming concepts to study towards the British Informatics Olympiad.

I plan to eventually solve every implementation question (part a) from past British Informatics Olympiad competitions and upload them to this repository, with detailed explanations of my solutions and/or directions to resources that would allow prospective competitors to understand how I have solved a problem.

The best way to improve at competitive programming -- like any skill -- is to practise. If you are here to prepare for future British Informatics Olympiads, good luck and happy programming!

## Resources I recommend 
- [Competitive Programmer's Handbook](https://cses.fi/book/book.pdf) by [Antti Laaksonen](https://github.com/pllk): This book explains just about every competitive programming concept that you'll encounter when preparing for most competitive programming competitions including the British Informatics Olympiad.
- [BIO Helper](https://www.britishinformatics.org): This is an insanely helpful website when it comes to preparing for the British Informatics Olympiad, has editorials and solutions to most past British Informatics Olympiad problems. However, to those not well-versed in competitive programming concepts, these may be hard to understand.
